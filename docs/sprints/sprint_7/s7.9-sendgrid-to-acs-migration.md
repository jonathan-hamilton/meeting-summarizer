## Business Justification

### Cost Analysis
Based on our email usage patterns for verification emails and meeting summary sharing:

| Monthly Volume | SendGrid Cost | ACS Cost | Annual Savings |
|----------------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| 10,000 emails  | $239.40      | $32.88   | **$206.52** (86%) |
| 25,000 emails  | $239.40      | $82.20   | **$157.20** (66%) |
| 50,000 emails  | $239.40      | $164.40  | **$75.00** (31%) |

### Strategic Benefits
- **Azure Ecosystem Unification**: Complete Azure-native architecture
- **Simplified Compliance**: Single cloud provider for all services
- **Unified Security Model**: Consistent with existing Azure Key Vault and Managed Identity
- **Consolidated Billing**: Single Azure invoice for all services
- **Pay-per-Use Model**: No monthly minimums, perfect for variable email volumes
