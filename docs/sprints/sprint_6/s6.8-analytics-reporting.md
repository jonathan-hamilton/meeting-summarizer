# Story S6.8: Analytics & Reporting Dashboard

## Overview
As a user and administrator, I want comprehensive analytics and reporting capabilities so that I can track meeting insights, usage patterns, and derive actionable business intelligence from meeting data.

**Status: PLANNED 📋**

**Completion Date:** TBD (Future Sprint)

**Dependencies**: S6.7 (API Integration & Third-Party Connectivity)

- User usage analytics and dashboard

**Phase 2 PLANNED**: Advanced Business Intelligence
- Meeting effectiveness analytics and scoring

### Database Schema
```sql
-- Analytics Tables
UserAnalytics (Id, UserId, Date, MeetingsProcessed, TokensUsed, ProcessingTime)
MeetingAnalytics (Id, MeetingId, Duration, ParticipantCount, SpeakingTimeDistribution, EngagementScore)
TeamAnalytics (Id, TeamId, Date, CollaborationScore, ProductivityMetrics, MeetingEffectiveness)
ActionItemAnalytics (Id, ActionItemId, CompletionTime, EfficiencyScore, FollowUpCount)

-- Reporting System
CustomReports (Id, UserId, Name, Configuration, ScheduleSettings, LastGenerated)
ReportSchedules (Id, ReportId, CronExpression, Recipients, Format, IsActive)
ReportGenerations (Id, ReportId, GeneratedDate, Status, FilePath, Recipients)
DashboardConfigurations (Id, UserId, Name, Layout, Widgets, IsDefault)

-- Metrics & KPIs
MetricsDefinitions (Id, Name, CalculationFormula, DataSources, UpdateFrequency)
KpiTargets (Id, UserId, MetricId, TargetValue, TimeFrame, TrackingPeriod)
MetricsHistory (Id, MetricId, Value, CalculatedDate, Context, Source)
```
