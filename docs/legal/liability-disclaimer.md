## 1. AI Technology Limitations

### Transcription Accuracy Disclaimer

**Automated Speech Recognition Limitations:**
- Our transcription service uses advanced AI technology (OpenAI Whisper), but **perfect accuracy is not guaranteed**
- Transcription quality depends on multiple factors outside our control:
  - **Audio Quality**: Background noise, echo, poor recording equipment
  - **Speech Characteristics**: Accents, speaking speed, pronunciation, volume
  - **Multiple Speakers**: Overlapping speech, speaker identification challenges
  - **Technical Terms**: Industry jargon, proper names, technical vocabulary
  - **Audio Format**: Compression artifacts, file corruption, format compatibility

**Expected Accuracy Ranges:**
- **High-quality single speaker**: 85-95% accuracy typical
- **Conference calls with multiple speakers**: 70-85% accuracy typical  
- **Poor audio quality or heavy accents**: 50-70% accuracy possible
- **Background noise or technical issues**: May result in significantly lower accuracy

**User Responsibility:**
- **Review all transcripts** for accuracy before relying on content
- **Verify important information** against original audio recordings
- **Use transcripts as starting points**, not definitive records
- **Report systematic accuracy issues** for service improvement

### AI Summarization Disclaimer

**Automated Content Analysis Limitations:**
- Our summarization service uses advanced language models (OpenAI GPT), but **interpretation limitations apply**
- AI summarization may not capture:
  - **Subtle Context**: Implied meanings, organizational context, relationship dynamics
  - **Emotional Nuance**: Tone, sarcasm, frustration, enthusiasm beyond explicit language
  - **Cultural Context**: Industry-specific practices, company culture, unspoken agreements
  - **Non-Verbal Communication**: Body language, visual presentations, shared documents
  - **Complex Negotiations**: Multi-party dynamics, conditional agreements, tentative decisions

**Content Risks:**
- **Bias Amplification**: AI models may reflect training data biases
- **Misinterpretation**: Complex discussions may be oversimplified or mischaracterized
- **Missing Information**: Important details may be omitted or deprioritized
- **False Emphasis**: AI may highlight less important points while missing key decisions
- **Context Loss**: Sequential meetings or ongoing projects may lack historical context

**User Responsibility:**
- **Review all summaries** against original transcripts and audio
- **Verify action items** and decisions with meeting participants
- **Use summaries as drafts** requiring human review and validation
- **Cross-reference important decisions** with multiple sources
