## 1. Introduction

Meeting Summary AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our AI-powered transcription and summarization platform.

**Key Commitments:**
- **Transparency**: Clear disclosure of all data collection and processing
- **User Control**: Full control over your data with easy deletion options  
- **Security**: Enterprise-grade protection using Microsoft Azure infrastructure
- **Compliance**: GDPR, CCPA, and international privacy law compliance
- **No Selling**: We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties

## 2. Information We Collect

- **Billing Information**: Payment method details, billing address (processed by secure payment providers)

- **Transcripts**: AI-generated text transcriptions of your audio content

### Usage & Technical Information
- **Service Usage**: Token consumption, processing history, feature usage statistics

- **Performance Data**: Processing times, system performance metrics (aggregated and anonymized)

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Analytics**: Page views, user interactions, feature adoption (anonymized)
- **Security Logs**: Login attempts, security events, fraud prevention data
- **Cookies**: Session management, user preferences, analytics (see Cookie Policy below)
