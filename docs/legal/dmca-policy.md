## 3. Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

### Requirements for Valid Notice

To file a copyright infringement claim, you must provide a written notice containing **ALL** of the following information:

#### **1. Identification of Copyrighted Work**
- A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed
- If multiple works are involved, provide a representative list
- Registration number if the work is registered with the Copyright Office

#### **2. Identification of Infringing Material**
- Specific identification of the material claimed to be infringing
- **Meeting Summary AI Context**: Provide the meeting transcript URL, meeting ID, or sufficient information to locate the specific audio file or transcript
- Description of where the infringing material is located on our platform

#### **3. Contact Information**
- Your full legal name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address
- If filing on behalf of another party, provide authorization documentation

#### **4. Good Faith Statement**
Include this exact statement:
> "I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."

#### **5. Accuracy and Authority Statement**
Include this exact statement:
> "The information in this notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or am authorized to act on behalf of the owner, of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed."

#### **6. Physical or Electronic Signature**
- Physical signature on printed notice, OR
- Electronic signature (typed name, digital signature, etc.)

### **Example DMCA Notice Format:**

```
To: dmca@meeting-summary.io
From: [Your contact information]
Date: [Current date]

DMCA Takedown Notice

1. Copyrighted Work:
   [Description of your copyrighted work]

2. Infringing Material:
   Meeting ID: [Meeting ID number]

3. Contact Information:
   Name: [Your full name]
```

## 4. Our Response to Valid DMCA Notices

### **Expeditious Response Process**

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

1. **Acknowledgment** (Within 24 hours)
   - Send confirmation of receipt to the complainant
   - Assign a case number for tracking

2. **Content Review** (Within 48 hours)
   - Review the allegedly infringing content
   - Verify the notice meets all DMCA requirements
   - Assess the validity of the copyright claim

3. **Takedown Action** (Within 72 hours for valid notices)
   - Remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing content
   - Notify the user who uploaded the content
   - Provide the user with the DMCA notice (minus personal contact details)
   - Inform the user of their counter-notification rights

4. **Documentation**
   - Maintain records of all DMCA notices and actions taken
   - Track repeat infringement for enforcement purposes

### **Invalid Notices**

If a DMCA notice is incomplete or invalid, we will:
- Notify the complainant of the deficiencies
- Provide guidance on how to submit a proper notice
- Not take action until a valid notice is received

## 5. Counter-Notification Process

### **User Rights**

If you believe your content was removed in error or due to misidentification, you may file a counter-notification.

### **Requirements for Valid Counter-Notice**

Your counter-notification must include **ALL** of the following:

#### **1. Identification of Removed Material**
- Description of the material that was removed
- Location where the material appeared before removal (Meeting ID, URL, etc.)

#### **2. Contact Information**
- Your full name, address, phone number, and email address

#### **3. Consent to Jurisdiction**
Include this exact statement:
> "I consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which my address is located, or if my address is outside the United States, the judicial district in which Meeting Summary AI is located, and I will accept service of process from the complainant."

#### **4. Good Faith Statement**
Include this exact statement:
> "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled."

#### **5. Physical or Electronic Signature**

### **Counter-Notice Response Process**

Upon receiving a valid counter-notification:

1. **Forward to Complainant** (Within 2-3 business days)
   - Send copy of counter-notice to original complainant
   - Inform them they have 10 business days to file a lawsuit

2. **Restore Content** (10-14 business days after sending counter-notice)
   - If no lawsuit is filed, restore the removed content
   - Notify the user that content has been restored
   - Resume normal service for the user

## 6. Repeat Infringer Policy

### **Three-Strike Policy**

We maintain a policy of terminating accounts of users who repeatedly infringe copyright:

#### **First Infringement**
- Content removed with DMCA notice
- User warned about copyright policy
- Account remains active

#### **Second Infringement**  
- Content removed with DMCA notice
- User receives final warning
- Account may be temporarily suspended
- Account remains active pending no further violations

#### **Third Infringement**
- **Account Termination**: Permanent account closure
- All user content permanently deleted
- User banned from creating new accounts
- No refund of unused tokens

### **Repeat Infringer Criteria**

A user is considered a repeat infringer if:
- They receive multiple valid DMCA takedown notices
- Counter-notifications are not filed, or are unsuccessful
- Pattern of uploading copyrighted content without authorization

### **Appeals Process**

Users may appeal repeat infringer determinations by:
- Emailing appeals@meeting-summary.io
- Providing evidence that takedowns were invalid
- Demonstrating good faith efforts to respect copyright

## 7. False Claims and Abuse

### **Penalties for False Claims**

Under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for:
- Attorney's fees and costs
- Damages suffered by the alleged infringer  
- Damages suffered by the service provider

### **Abuse Prevention**

We reserve the right to:
- Reject DMCA notices from parties with a history of false claims
- Request additional verification for questionable notices
- Report patterns of abuse to appropriate authorities
- Block or limit access from persistent bad actors

## 8. Limitations and Exclusions

### **Safe Harbor Protection**

This policy is designed to comply with the DMCA safe harbor provisions under 17 U.S.C. § 512. We:
- Do not actively monitor user content for copyright infringement
- Respond expeditiously to valid takedown notices
- Maintain and enforce a repeat infringer policy  
- Do not interfere with standard technical measures used by copyright owners

### **Content We Don't Control**

Our DMCA policy applies only to content hosted on Meeting Summary AI platforms:
- **Included**: User-uploaded audio files, generated transcripts, summaries
- **Excluded**: Third-party websites, external links, embedded content from other services

### **International Copyright**

While this policy focuses on U.S. DMCA law, we respect copyright laws globally and may:
- Respond to valid takedown requests under other national copyright laws
- Cooperate with international copyright enforcement agencies
- Apply similar procedures for non-U.S. copyright claims
